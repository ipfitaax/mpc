About MPC

Practical IT Training for Your Professional Future

Mogadishu Professional Certificate provides practical training in information technology and professional skills. Our goal is to help learners understand essential IT concepts, practise useful technical tasks, complete meaningful projects, and prepare for the next stage of their education or career.

MPC connects classroom learning with real-world application. Students should not only memorize definitions; they learn how to approach a technical problem, use appropriate tools, explain their work, and continue learning after the program ends.

MPC waxay isku xirtaa waxbarashada fasalka iyo adeegsiga nolosha dhabta ah. Ardaygu ma xifdiyo qeexid keliya — wuxuu bartaa sida loo xalliyo dhibaato farsamo, loo isticmaalo qalabka saxda ah, loona sharaxo shaqadiisa.

Who Is MPC For?

Your Next Step After High School

MPC is designed for students and young adults who want to build a practical professional skill in information technology. You may be a recent high-school graduate, a learner changing career direction, or someone who wants structured IT training before entering the workplace.

You do not need to know everything before you begin. The program takes students from essential computer knowledge to practical IT tasks in a clear sequence. You receive guidance, opportunities to practise, and support in preparing for your first professional opportunity.

Two Clear Pathways

Choose the Program That Matches Your Goal

MPC offers two study pathways for learners beginning an IT career. Both include practical learning and career preparation. The one-year program provides broader development and more time for practice. The six-month program provides a focused, intensive route for students who want to build essential skills in a shorter period.

Best for broader preparation

One-Year Professional IT Skills Program

Build a Complete Foundation for an IT Career

Designed for recent high-school graduates and beginners who want time to learn, practise, and develop confidence. Students progress through essential IT subjects, complete practical projects, and receive structured preparation for employment or further certification.

  • A progressive foundation for beginners.
  • Practical computer, networking, support, and data skills.
  • Guided assignments and portfolio projects.
  • Professional communication and workplace preparation.
  • CV, interview, and job-search guidance.
Explore the One-Year Program
Best for a faster start

Six-Month Intensive IT Skills Program

Learn Essential IT Skills in Six Focused Months

Designed for learners who want a concentrated route into practical IT training. The program focuses on the essential knowledge and hands-on tasks students need to begin building a portfolio and preparing for entry-level opportunities.

  • Focused training for faster progress.
  • Essential computer, support, networking, and digital-work skills.
  • Practical assignments instead of theory alone.
  • Career-readiness activities and application guidance.
  • Suitable for students who can commit to an intensive schedule.

How We Teach

Learn by Doing It

IT is a practical profession. At MPC students learn concepts and then use them. Lessons include demonstrations, exercises, troubleshooting tasks, small projects, group activities, and opportunities to explain technical work clearly.

You finish the program with more than a certificate. You have evidence of what you can do: project files, reports, diagrams, presentations, and a portfolio that supports future applications.

Career Preparation

We Help You Prepare for the Workplace

Completing training is an important step, but beginning a career also requires confidence, communication, evidence of ability, and a plan. MPC supports students as they prepare to present themselves professionally and search for suitable opportunities.

Our career support helps students prepare a CV, organize a practical portfolio, practise interviews, improve professional communication, identify suitable entry-level roles, and understand how to submit and follow up on applications.

Possible Entry-Level Directions
