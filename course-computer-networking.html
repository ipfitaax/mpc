Computer Networking Fundamentals

Understand how networks connect devices, and learn to set up, configure, and troubleshoot them.

Duration: 12 weeks Format: In-person, Soobe Level: Beginner

What You'll Learn

Network types and topologies

IP addressing and subnetting basics

Routers, switches, and cabling

Wireless networking and troubleshooting
